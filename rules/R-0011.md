## Rule

Any degradation - demo mode, cached keys, a smaller model - must be visible in the output,
not just in a log line. A silent fallback is a lie.

## Why

1. DMS_DEMO_FALLBACK served demo answers with live badge
2. Stale process served old config while fresh tests passed

## Check

Assert user-visible badge/mode field matches actual execution path.
