## Rule

A skipped test is a failing test. Make suites fail loudly with the cause named.
Never skip on a lock, a missing service, or a timeout without failing the run.

## Why

1. Adversarial suite skipped on DuckDB lock PID and reported green
2. Missing live stack treated as pass instead of explicit failure

## Check

Search for `@pytest.mark.skip`, `pytest.skip(`, `skipif` without fail-on-lock policy.
