## Rule

**Corpus text** (titles, bodies, incident strings, templates): laptop-ASCII only.
Never use em dash, en dash, >=/<=/arrow glyphs, curly quotes, or ellipsis characters.
Use plain substitutes: ` - ` or `--`, `>=`, `<=`, `->`, `'`, `"`, `...`.

**CLI scripts** call `ensure_utf8_stdio()` before `print()` (`scripts/stdio_utf8.py`).
Do not rely on `PYTHONUTF8=1` alone.

**Agents** writing rules, workflows, findings, attacks, or skills must follow this forever.
`kb.py validate` rejects forbidden characters.

## Why

1. `kb.py search` crashed on Windows when a title contained `>=` as a Unicode glyph
2. Fancy punctuation is not on a US keyboard and breaks cp1252 consoles and copy-paste
3. A tool that dies on its own corpus is a silent gate failure (R-0011)

## Check

`python scripts/kb.py validate` passes.
`python scripts/kb.py search "n>=300" --limit 5` exits 0 on Windows.
