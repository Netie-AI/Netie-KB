## Rule

A control that refuses legitimate work is a failure, not a win.
After any hardening, re-run the full corpus to confirm nothing valid became refused.

## Why

1. `delim=';'` treated as path traversal
2. `generate_series()` caught by overly broad default-deny

## Check

Run hostile SQL corpus + golden eval after every manifest/enforcer change. New refusals need justification.
