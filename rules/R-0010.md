## Rule

Zero errors in n trials bounds the true error rate at 3/n (95% rule of three).
Do not claim "0 wrong" or "<1% wrong" below n=300.

## Why

1. 19/19 reported as "0 confidently wrong"
2. Trust UI `supported` flag would lie at small N

## Check

Report N and wrong count. Refuse supported claims until corpus N>=310 and wrong==0.
