## Rule

Every gate asserts on the artifact the customer receives, at the layer they receive it from.
Assertions on intermediate artifacts are necessary and insufficient.

## Why

1. `python -m importlinter` exited 0 running zero contracts
2. Exclusion tests asserted generated SQL, never the answer text
3. A stale process served demo mode while tests passed against a fresh one
4. Adversarial suite skipped on DuckDB lock and reported green
5. C10 measured Cortex; the badge defect was in the DMS envelope mapping

## Check

If a test asserts on SQL, config, an internal object, or a log line - ask what the customer sees, and assert that too.
