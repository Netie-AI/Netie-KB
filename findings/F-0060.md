## Expected vs actual

**Expected:** a ticket closes only after a different-run Verify says YES on the covering
oid, plus real push CI.

**Actual:** PR #214 merged at 15:52:58Z and #211 closed at 15:53:00Z with no Verify
record, although the PR body itself said "Closes nothing. Do not close #211 from this
agent." Push CI was genuinely green (1922 passed). The later independent Verify read
AC1 PARTIAL, AC2 NO, AC3 NO, AC4 PARTIAL, AC5 YES. The next seat in the order (#212)
would have built its CoT climb on a measured route that cannot fire.

## Reproduction

1. `gh api repos/Netie-AI/Cortex/issues/211/timeline` - closed event at 15:53:00Z, no
   comment before the Verify comment.
2. `gh pr view 214 --json mergedAt,mergeCommit` - 15:52:58Z, ceb7fc2.
3. Verify lenses on ceb7fc2: issue comment 5654756341.

## Root cause class

Closure bound to an intermediate artifact (a merged PR plus green CI) instead of the
acceptance verdict. Same class as R-0001, applied to ticket state.

## Invariant gap

R-0003 has no enforcement point on the close path. The close actor cannot be told apart
from PR Bot acting as the founder account, so the gap is the missing gate, not the actor.

## Notes

Cost: one reopen, one follow-up PR, and three days of #212 blocked. A ticket whose PR
body says "do not close" is a cheap signal to honour.
